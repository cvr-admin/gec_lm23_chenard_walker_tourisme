# 1923 GEC Chenard & Walcker 2L Tourisme

Requires Custom Shaders Patch (CSP). The car simulates stalling, cooling, oil and fuel pressure, wear, and pit and roadside repairs.

## Extra buttons

Bind these under **Content Manager → Settings → Assetto Corsa → Controls → Patch**.

- **A** — Hold to start or restart. Select neutral or press the clutch first.
- **B** — While stopped, start pit service in your pit box or available roadside repairs elsewhere.
- **E / F** — Close / open the radiator shutters one step.
- **P** — Rescue push when nearly stationary. Neutral or 1st pushes forward, reverse pushes back, 2nd pushes right, and 3rd pushes left.
- **C / G** — Oil hand pump / fuel-tank air pump. Enable the respective manual option in car setup to take over from the riding mechanic.

## Realism and apps

Edit [`extension/realism_settings.ini`](extension/realism_settings.ini) with `1` for on or `0` for off, then restart the session. Server overrides take priority online.

**GEC Driver manual** gives driving and fault prompts, **GEC Dashboard** shows gauges and warnings, and **CVR Pit Crew** lets you select and start specific repairs.
